Collection I — Bespoke

Faceté

The Facet Collection — cut like stone, soft like skin. Four studies of closeness, available by private commission only.

Faceté takes its form from the cut of a diamond — hard angles that catch a single, deliberate light. Each canvas holds a different closeness: a breath before a kiss, a hand finding skin, the quiet after. Not reproduced, not repeated. Each piece is painted once, for one collector, and is never offered again.

Collection II

Éclat

The Éclat Collection — abstract works finished with real gold leaf, eight canvases.

Éclat is French for a burst of light — the moment gold catches and throws it back. Each canvas is built in layers of oil and pigment, then broken open with hand-applied gold leaf, so the surface shifts as you move past it. No two catch the light the same way.

Collection III

Lunaire

Six diptychs. Twelve paintings. Luxury rendered in half-light.

Lunaire moves in pairs, as the moon does — each diptych a study in opposition and completion, light held against shadow, one half incomplete without the other. Across six diptychs, the collection traces the moon's quiet phases: waxing and waning, concealment and reveal. Abstract, restrained, and deliberately unhurried.

Collection IV

Solaire

Six triptychs. Eighteen paintings. The full arc of a single day's light.

Where Lunaire holds stillness, Solaire moves through time. Each triptych unfolds across three panels — dawn, noon, dusk — a single arc of light captured in three distinct moments. Six triptychs form a complete solar cycle: bolder, warmer, and more expansive than its lunar counterpart. Solaire is Lunaire's celestial opposite — the sun to its moon, the full arc to its quiet phase.
